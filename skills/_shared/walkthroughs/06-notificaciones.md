# Notifications Setup / Configuración de Notificaciones

**EN:** Choose how your automation/agent notifies you when it finishes running. Pick the one that fits your setup.

**ES:** Elige cómo tu automatización/agente te avisa cuando termina de correr. Elige el que se adapte a tu setup.

---

## Option 1 — Pushover (recommended for push notifications)

**EN:** Pushover sends push notifications to your iPhone or Android. One-time payment of $4.99 per platform. Best option for real-time alerts that feel like a message.

**ES:** Pushover manda notificaciones push a tu iPhone o Android. Pago único de $4.99 por plataforma. Mejor opción para alertas en tiempo real que se sienten como un mensaje.

**Setup:**
1. Download the Pushover app: [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/pushover-notifications/id506088175) / [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.superblock.pushover)
2. Create an account at https://pushover.net
3. Copy your **User Key** from the dashboard
4. Go to https://pushover.net/apps/build → create an app → copy the **API Token**
5. Paste both keys in the chat when asked

**Cost:** $4.99 one-time (no subscription) ✅

---

## Option 2 — Email (free, no extra setup)

**EN:** Send results to any email address using a free service like Resend or EmailJS. Slightly less immediate than push notifications but requires no extra app.

**ES:** Manda resultados a cualquier email usando un servicio gratuito como Resend o EmailJS. Ligeramente menos inmediato que las notificaciones push pero no requiere ninguna app extra.

**Setup (Resend):**
1. Go to https://resend.com and create a free account
2. Go to **API Keys** → **Create API Key**
3. Copy the key
4. Paste it in the chat when asked
5. You can send to any email address

**Cost:** Free (100 emails/day on free plan) ✅

---

## Option 3 — No notification (results only in Notion)

**EN:** Skip notifications entirely. Results are saved to Notion and you check them whenever you want. Best if you don't need real-time alerts.

**ES:** Sin notificaciones. Los resultados se guardan en Notion y los revisas cuando quieras. Mejor si no necesitas alertas en tiempo real.

**Cost:** $0 ✅
